SPACEMANTIS

A roguelike space-career game for M5Stack Core2

The premise

You are lost in space. There is no galaxy map and no plotted course. You fly, encounter things, make opportunistic decisions, and use gates to dive through subspace and resurface somewhere else.

When you are destroyed, the universe is wiped. Your pilot, career and fitted capabilities survive. Most place knowledge does not.

The flight view

  • Touch drag — yaw / roll; it never drags the sky.
  • Tilt — steer relative to your captured flight attitude.
  • Fly gates — labeled gates are things you have heard about; unknown gates are pure discovery.
  • Three gates — consecutive gates commit a dive/resurface.
  • Deeper space — increasingly strange visuals, anomalies and persistent landmarks.

The verbs

HAIL
Parley, trade, bluff, receive rumors.
ATTACK
Simple combat resolve.
DOCK
Auto-approach a station and commit.
DISCOVER
Fly into unknown gates and landmarks.

Install on Core2

Chrome or Edge with a USB data connection.
