SPACEMANTIS

A roguelike space-career game for M5Stack Core2

by BasaltSoftWorks

Lost in space. No map. Dive deeper.

You fly, encounter ships and giants, commit through gates, and resurface somewhere else. When the hull goes, your escape pod is recovered far away — ranks and fit survive; the sky does not.

The premise

There is no galaxy map and no plotted course. Gates are things you have heard about — or pure discovery. Deeper subspace gets stranger. Chart a place once and its name can outlive the universe you lost.

The flight view

  • Touch drag — look around (yaw / pitch).
  • Tilt — aim; twist for roll.
  • Gates & portals — thread rings, dive under, climb back.
  • Verbs — hail, attack, dock, mine, scoop, chart.

The verbs

HAIL
Parley, trade, bluff, rumors.
ATTACK
Short combat resolve.
DOCK
Station board, work, gear.
CHART
Deep landmarks that can persist.

Install on Core2

Chrome or Edge with a USB data cable.
